Where the group operates.

Five companies, four kinds of business: things we build, things we move, things we finance, and things we run.

Build

Construction, infrastructure, and property development — Tycoon Plus delivers the physical assets the rest of the group, and the public, depend on.

Power

Tycoon Oil and Gas generates, distributes, and finances power — from independent grid capacity to solar mini-grids for underserved sites.

Move

Tycoon Motors runs haulage, warehousing, and last-mile freight, carrying goods for the group's own businesses and for outside clients.

House

Tycoon Homes operates boutique hotels and serviced apartments in the cities where the group already has a presence.

Finance

Joibslink manages the group's own capital and offers trade finance and asset management to companies outside the group.

Approach

Capital without a head office breathing down anyone's neck.

Tycoon Group does not run the companies it owns. Each one keeps its own leadership, its own name in the market, and its own targets. The group's job is to fund the next site, the next fleet, the next hire, and to move capital between companies when one needs it faster than a bank would give it.

That structure only works with discipline. Every company reports the same way, on the same schedule, so capital can move on facts rather than favourites.

Ownership

Tycoon Group holds a controlling stake in every company on the board — never a passive minority position.

Horizon

Capital is committed for the long run. No holding is built, or bought, to be flipped inside five years.

Autonomy

Operating decisions stay with each company's own management. The group office sets reporting standards, not strategy.

How it was built.

Fourteen years, one company at a time.

2011

Tycoon Plus builds the hotel

A large edifice of hospitality in Ebonyi, with competitive room rate and beautiful rooms

2016

Tycoon Motors launches

Built first to move materials between the group's own sites, then opened to outside freight clients within a year.

2019

Joibslink is established

The group's internal treasury function is spun out into a standalone finance company, and "Tycoon Group" becomes the formal holding structure.

2024

The group crosses its first border

Tycoon Oil and Gas and Tycoon Motors begin operations in Ghana, followed by a Joibslink office in the UAE.
